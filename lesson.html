C# Learning Modules

Welcome to the CIE-Sharp learning modules. These lessons are designed to help ICT students understand the fundamental concepts of the C# programming language before playing interactive microgames.

Learning Objective

This instructional material aims to help ICT students strengthen their understanding of the basic concepts of C# programming through lesson-based learning and interactive microgames.
