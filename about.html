WHAT IS CIE-SHARP?

A Learning Material Designed Around Practice

CIE-Sharp combines learning content with short, interactive activities to make C# syntax practice more engaging and manageable.

🎮

Microgame-Based Learning

Learners practice C# syntax through short and focused activities rather than relying only on traditional reading materials.

💻

Web-Based

CIE-Sharp is designed as a web-based learning material that can provide accessible and interactive programming practice.

🎯

Focused Practice

Activities focus on C# syntax formatting and gradually provide learners with opportunities to apply what they have learned.

OUR STORY

From a Learning Need to CIE-Sharp

CIE-Sharp was developed as a response to the need for a more interactive way of supporting ICT students while learning programming concepts.

Instead of creating another traditional reviewer, the project explored the use of short interactive microgames that allow learners to practice, make mistakes, receive feedback, and try again.

Through the development process, the concept evolved into a web-based learning material focused on C# syntax formatting and progressive practice.

01

Identify

Identify the learning need and challenges related to practicing C# programming syntax.

02

Design

Plan a learning material that combines lessons, activities, challenges, and feedback.

03

Develop

Build the CIE-Sharp web-based microgame and its interactive learning activities.

04

Improve

Refine the learning material based on testing, feedback, and observed learning needs.

WHY MICROGAME?

What Makes CIE-Sharp a Microgame?

CIE-Sharp uses short and focused interactive challenges to turn syntax practice into an active learning experience.

01

Short Challenges

Activities are focused on specific learning tasks rather than long programming sessions.

02

Progressive Difficulty

Challenges can progress from beginner activities toward more challenging syntax tasks.

03

Immediate Feedback

Learners receive feedback after attempting an activity and can identify areas for correction.

04

Retry and Practice

Learners can repeat activities to strengthen their understanding and improve their performance.
